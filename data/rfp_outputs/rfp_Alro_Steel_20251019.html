1. Introduction

Introduction

Nexa is a mid-sized enterprise specializing in the procurement and supply chain management of aluminum sheets. Our operations are extensive and require a consistent supply of high-quality materials to maintain our business continuity and meet our clients' needs.

We are currently facing a business need for a reliable and qualified supplier for 6061-T6 Aluminum Sheet. This material is critical to our operations and its consistent quality, timely delivery, and competitive pricing are paramount to our business success.

Our strategic goal is to establish a long-term strategic partnership with a supplier who can meet our specifications and requirements. Specifically, we are looking to partner with Alro Steel to supply 6061-T6 Aluminum Sheet to our Buffalo, New York location. Our target budget ranges from $50 to $400 and we expect the delivery to commence by October 18, 2025.

In terms of historical context, we have been evaluating Alro Steel based on their expertise in aluminum sheets. They are a known supplier of a wide variety of metals and plastics and offer local delivery in Buffalo. We believe that their experience and capabilities align with our business needs and objectives.

This Request for Proposal (RFP) is intended to solicit proposals from qualified suppliers who can meet our requirements and help us achieve our strategic goals. We look forward to receiving your detailed proposal outlining how you can meet our needs and contribute to our business success.

5. Evaluation Criteria

Evaluation Criteria

The selection of the successful proposal will be based on a comprehensive, structured evaluation process. Proposals will be assessed according to the following weighted criteria:

  1. Technical Capability (30%): The proposal should demonstrate the bidder's ability to meet the technical requirements of the project. This includes the ability to deliver the specified product, 6061-T6 Aluminum Sheet, to Buffalo, New York. The bidder's technical expertise, resources, and approach to the project will be evaluated under this criterion.

  2. Pricing Competitiveness (30%): The proposal should provide a detailed and competitive pricing structure. The pricing must be within the budget range of $50 - $400. The value for money offered by the proposal will be evaluated under this criterion.

  3. Delivery Reliability (20%): The proposal should demonstrate the bidder's ability to deliver the product reliably and on time. The bidder's past performance, delivery methods, and contingency plans will be evaluated under this criterion.

  4. Quality Assurance (15%): The proposal should demonstrate the bidder's commitment to quality assurance. The bidder must meet quality/certification standards, specifically ISO 9001. The bidder's quality control processes, certifications, and commitment to continuous improvement will be evaluated under this criterion.

  5. Customer Service (5%): The proposal should demonstrate the bidder's commitment to providing excellent customer service. The bidder's approach to customer service, including communication methods, response times, and problem-solving capabilities, will be evaluated under this criterion.

Required vs. Optional Capabilities

All the capabilities mentioned above are required. Proposals that do not meet these requirements will be deemed non-responsive and will not be considered for further evaluation.

Non-Negotiables and Qualitative Factors

The following are non-negotiable requirements for this RFP:

  • Must be able to deliver to Buffalo, New York.

6. Pricing Details

Section IV: Pricing Details

The purpose of this section is to provide clear instructions on how vendors should structure their pricing. It is crucial that all vendors adhere to the same pricing structure to ensure a fair and efficient evaluation process.

A. Pricing Format and Currency

All prices should be presented in a detailed line-item breakdown format. This means that each cost should be itemized separately, with the unit costs and quantities clearly indicated. The pricing should be provided in United States Dollars (USD).

B. Contract Length Expectations

The contract length for this project is expected to be 1 year. Please ensure that your pricing reflects this duration.

C. Required Cost Categories

Vendors are required to provide pricing for the following cost categories:

  1. Unit price for 6061-T6 Aluminum Sheet
  2. Shipping and logistics to Buffalo, New York
  3. Taxes and duties
  4. Quality inspection fees (if applicable)
  5. Rush delivery fees (if applicable)

Please note that the costs for each of these categories should be itemized separately in your pricing proposal.

D. Volume Discounts

If applicable, vendors should also provide information on any volume discounts that may be available. These should be clearly outlined and easy to understand.

E. Budget Range and Payment Terms

The budget range for this project is between $50 and $400. Please ensure that your pricing falls within this range. The payment terms are Net 30 days from delivery and acceptance.

In conclusion, your pricing proposal should be clear, detailed, and standardized.
